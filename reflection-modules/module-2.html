Nationalism & Citizenship Training

True citizenship is lived every day. It’s not about knowing the Bill of Rights. It’s about putting into practice the values of Maka-Diyos, Maka-Tao, Maka-Bayan and Maka-Kalikasan. These values shape who we are. They turn what we know into how we act. Leadership doesn’t start with speeches. It starts with choices. It starts with showing up. It starts with doing the thing even when no one is watching. Being responsible, in democracy means voting, listening, speaking up and helping others. It means showing up for your community your country and your world. Every day every decision matters.
